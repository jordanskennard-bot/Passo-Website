passoagency.com/How it works

We start with a full check and then watch every month.

You give us read-only access to your ad accounts. Nothing changes in them unless you choose Advanced and approve each change.

  1. Connect your accounts

    Give us read-only access to Google Ads and Meta. Connect Shopify or GA4 as well, so we can check the sales each platform claims against your own.

  2. Get your health check

    Within three working days you get every finding in your accounts with the action to take. The most important come first, grouped by when to act and who should act. For now the health check is by invitation.

    See a sample health check

  3. Each month on Basics

    We recheck your accounts and report what worked and what did not. New findings and alerts arrive as they happen, and we confirm the fixes you made. Every recommendation comes with its reasoning and what it should be worth.

  4. On Advanced we make the changes

    We make each change once you approve it. We run tests to show what works and report what our changes delivered against our fee.

What we never do

  • Change anything in your accounts without your approval
  • Sell or share your data
  • Take payment from Google or Meta

The detail of what we read from each platform is on our data and privacy page.
