Passo is run by Jordan Kennard.

I have spent my career buying media on the brand side.

At PlayStation I built the in-house team that buys display and video ads across websites and apps, and I led global performance media from 2020 to 2024 with a team in London and San Francisco. At Native Instruments I was Director of Paid Media across Native Instruments, iZotope and Plugin Alliance, with a team in Berlin, London, Boston and Madrid.

I built Passo to give advertisers independent advice. It checks your accounts every month and shows its working, so you can judge it for yourself.
