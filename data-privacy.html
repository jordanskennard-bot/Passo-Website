Data & privacy

What we read, why, and how it’s protected.

Plain-English explanation of every external data source we connect to. If you are reviewing this page on behalf of a platform, Google, Meta, or Shopify, this is the canonical reference for what we do with your merchants’ data. The legal policy lives at /privacy.

The Passo data pact

We read by default, change only what you approve, and we never resell.

We always

  • Read only by default. On our Entry plan every call we make to Google Ads, Merchant Center, Google Analytics and Meta is read-only. We never create, edit, pause or delete anything in your accounts.
  • Ask before any change. On our Advanced plan, Passo makes changes in your ad accounts only after you approve each one in the portal, within limits you agree, and every change is logged and can be reversed.
  • Honour disconnect immediately. When you disconnect a source or uninstall the Shopify app, we delete its access token the same day and stop collecting data from it.

We never

  • Resell, export or share your Shopify, Google or Meta data with any third party, except the processors named in our privacy policy that help us run the service.
  • Collect your customers’ personal details. We do not read the names, email addresses, phone numbers or postal addresses of your store’s customers.
  • Send credentials to the browser. Access and refresh tokens are encrypted, held server-side only and never logged.

Google Ads

We connect to Google Ads through Google’s standard OAuth 2.0 flow with the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/adwords scope. Every API call is read-only, made server-side from a Vercel-hosted worker using the official google-ads-api Node client against the latest stable Google Ads API.

What we read

Read-only

Resources customer, customer_client, campaign, ad_group, ad_group_ad, keyword_view, search_term_view, product_group_view, shopping_performance_view, change_event, geo_target_constant.

Methods CustomerService.listAccessibleCustomers, GoogleAdsService.search / searchStream, GeoTargetConstantService.suggestGeoTargetConstants.

Why So our health check and reports can show your campaign structure, surface the search queries that triggered your ads, tie Shopping and Performance Max spend back to specific Shopify SKUs, comment on recent changes to your account, reconcile Google-attributed conversions against Shopify orders, and compute channel-level cost per acquisition.

What we do not call

Excluded

No mutate* method on any service under our current access. That includes CampaignService.mutateCampaigns, CampaignBudgetService.mutateCampaignBudgets, AdGroupAdService.mutateAdGroupAds, RecommendationService.applyRecommendation, and every other write call.

If you choose our Advanced plan, where Passo applies changes you approve, we will request write access separately and update this page before any change is made. The full breakdown of API resources, methods, and rate-limit budgets is on our how it works page.

Storage The OAuth refresh token is AES-256 encrypted at rest in Supabase Postgres (eu-west-2). The developer token is a server-side environment secret, never sent to the browser, never logged. Report data is kept while you are a client and deleted within 30 days after the relationship ends or you disconnect.

Compliance Use of Google Ads data follows the Google Ads API Required Minimum Functionality policy for reporting tools and the Google Ads API Terms of Service. Disclosure of how we process Google Ads data is included in our privacy policy.

Meta Ads

Connected only when you choose to, by signing in with Facebook and approving access to the ad accounts you select. Every call is made server-side through Meta’s Marketing API. You can remove our access at any time from your Meta Business settings or from the Passo portal.

What we read

Read-only

Data Ad account structure (campaigns, ad sets, ads), settings, spend, Meta-reported conversions and performance insights, creative details, and product catalogue status.

Permissions ads_read and business_management, the latter used only to see and link the ad accounts you choose to share.

Why So our health check and reports can show how your account is set up, compare what Meta reports against the sales your Shopify store recorded, and turn the gap into plain-English findings and recommendations. Competitor creative comes from the public Ad Library and needs no access to your account.

Changes, only with your approval

Gated

On the Entry plan we call no write method. You see recommendations and make any changes in Meta Ads Manager yourself.

If you choose the Advanced plan, we will ask for ads_management separately, and only then. Each change is shown to you in the portal and made only after you approve it, within budget limits you set, with a log of every change and a way to reverse it.

We never request leads_retrieval or any permission that reads consumer data beyond campaign performance. We never post on your Facebook Page or Instagram account.

Storage The Meta access token is AES-256 encrypted at rest and deleted when you disconnect. Report data is kept while you are a client and deleted within 30 days after the relationship ends or you disconnect.

Compliance Use of Meta data follows the Meta Platform Terms and Meta’s developer policies. Passo Agency Ltd operates the app from its own verified business portfolio with the domain passoagency.com. Disclosure of how we process Meta data is included in our privacy policy.

Google Merchant Center

Optional. Connected through Google OAuth with the read-only content scope. We read product data, feed status and product disapprovals so our health check can find products missing from Shopping ads and explain how to fix them.

Google Analytics

Optional. Connected via the GA4 Data API with the read-only analytics scope. We use it to triangulate paid-channel performance against on-site engagement so our reports can comment on landing-page conversion rates by source. If you skip the connection we use Shopify and ad-account data only.

Shopify

Connected when you install the Passo app from the Shopify App Store, under the access you approve at install. We read orders, products and shop details through the Shopify Admin API. Orders are the source of truth for revenue, against which what advertising platforms report is compared. We do not read your customers’ names, email addresses, phone numbers or postal addresses. App charges are billed by Shopify on your Shopify invoice, so we never see card details.

Where the data lives

Data is stored in a Supabase-managed Postgres database in eu-west-2 (London), encrypted at rest. Application servers run on Vercel. Some processing, such as AI analysis, is carried out by the providers named in our privacy policy, under data transfer safeguards approved for the UK.

Backups are encrypted and retained for 30 days. If you uninstall the Shopify app, your store data, access tokens and backups are deleted within 30 days of Shopify’s deletion request. We keep only a de-identified record of recommendations and their outcomes, with anything that could identify you removed.
