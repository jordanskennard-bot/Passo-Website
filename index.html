The ad platforms mark their own homework.

Google and Meta set your bids, choose your placements and count the sales they claim. We check every claim against your own sales and recommend what we have seen work in accounts like yours.

We take no money from Google, Meta or any other ad platform.

We check what every pound of your spend is doing.

  1. I

    Wasted spend

    Searches, audiences, sites and apps that cost money and rarely sell.

  2. II

    Customers you already have

    How much of your budget goes on people who have already bought from you.

  3. III

    Sales you can trust

    Whether Google and Meta count each sale once and how many of those sales the ads caused.

  4. IV

    Settings that favour the platform

    Automatic features the platforms switch on and changes they made to your ads, audiences and budgets. We check whether each one works for you.

  5. V

    Ads that still sell

    Whether you have enough new ads running and which ones have worn out.

  6. VI

    Room to grow

    Campaigns that sell well and run out of budget and targets that hold back sales.

  7. VII

    What to do first

    Every finding comes with the action, who should take it and what it should be worth. One link sends it all to whoever runs your ads.

Every recommendation shows its working, its worth and how we will prove it.

Each recommendation says why we make it, what it should bring in or save and how we'll verify it.

Testing is hard and often skipped. A reliable test needs sound statistics and enough spend to give an answer, and judging both takes years of buying media.

For every recommendation we lay out how we'll prove the impact and confirm what is realistic before you begin. Each result shapes what we recommend next, so your media runs on what you have proved.
