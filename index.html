What we do

Start with a free check, then choose how much help you want.

Install the Passo app from the Shopify App Store and connect your Google account. Everything you get appears inside the app in your Shopify admin.

01

Free check

A health check of your Google Ads account, written for owners new to advertising. The top fixes come first, each with the reason and what to do.

02

Basics

A monthly report, your top recommendations each month and alerts when something changes. You make the changes in your own accounts.

03

Advanced

Everything in Basics, and we make the changes you approve, run tests to prove what works and talk it through with you each month. Advanced is sold directly for now; email us.

Data sources

We read only what the check needs.

Every connection is read only and you can disconnect it at any time. We never resell your data. The full detail is on our data and privacy page.

Shopify
Orders, products and shop details through the Shopify Admin API, so we can compare what advertising platforms report with your actual sales. We do not read customer names, emails or addresses.
Google Ads
Campaign structure, cost and conversion figures through the Google Ads API. We do not create, edit, pause or delete anything in your account. Full detail at how we use your Google Ads data.
Merchant Center
Product status and feed problems that stop your products appearing in Shopping ads.
Google Analytics
Optional. Landing pages and sessions, to check what happens after someone clicks an ad.
Meta Ads
Coming soon. The connection opens once Meta has finished reviewing our app.
