Who can use the service

The service is for businesses. You may use it if you are aged 18 or over and have the authority to accept these terms for the business you represent. You do not need a Shopify store: any business with a Google Ads or Meta ad account can use Passo. Consumer protection rules that apply only to individuals do not apply to this agreement.

When the agreement starts

The agreement starts when you accept these terms. That happens when you use a health check code, choose a plan in the portal or the Shopify app, sign an order form or accept a consulting proposal. It continues until it ends under the section on ending the agreement.

What each plan includes

Health check. A one-off review of your Google Ads and Meta accounts, given free by invitation code. We read your accounts and change nothing in them.

Basics. A monthly recheck of your accounts, a monthly report, every recommendation and alerts. We read your accounts and change nothing in them. You decide whether to act on our recommendations and you make any change yourself.

Advanced. Everything in Basics. We also make changes in your ad accounts once you have approved them. Every change we make is logged and we can reverse a change if you ask.

Consulting. A project with a scope, deliverables and fee set out in a written proposal.

What each plan includes is described on our pricing page at the time you choose it. We may improve the service over time. We will not remove a main feature of a plan you have paid for during the period you have paid for.

Access to your accounts

When you connect an account you give us permission to read it for as long as the connection is live. You keep ownership of every account and of the data in it.

On Advanced you also give us permission to make changes in the accounts you name. We ask for that permission separately and tell you what it covers before it takes effect. We make a change only after you approve it in the portal. Approvals can be given for one change at a time or within limits you set in writing.

You can disconnect any account at any time. When you do, we stop reading from it and revoke our access on the same day.

Your responsibilities

You remain the advertiser for every account you connect. You are responsible for your ads, your products, your budgets and your spend, and for following the advertising policies of each platform.

You agree to give us accurate information, to tell us about changes to your business that affect our advice and to review changes we propose before you approve them.

You agree not to use the service to advertise anything unlawful in the United Kingdom or in a market where your ads run. You agree not to copy, reverse engineer or interfere with the service. You agree not to try to reach another client's data or use the service to build a competing product.

Fees and billing

Our fees are shown on the pricing page and exclude VAT, which we add where it applies. Your advertising spend is not part of our fees: you pay Google, Meta and any other platform directly.

Basics bought in the Shopify app is billed by Shopify on your Shopify invoice. Shopify sets the charge in US dollars and shows you its equivalent in your billing currency.

If your business uses Shopify, you buy Basics only in the Shopify app, and Shopify bills it. Advanced is not yet offered to Shopify stores.

Everything else, including Advanced, consulting and Basics for businesses not on Shopify, is invoiced by us in pounds. Invoices are payable within 14 days unless an order form or proposal says otherwise.

Yearly billing. If you choose to pay yearly, you pay for 12 months upfront at the yearly price shown on the pricing page.

Creative production. Images and video we produce for you are billed per piece. Costs from creators and other suppliers are passed on at cost. We will agree these with you before any cost is incurred.

Price changes. We may change our fees on 30 days' written notice. A change applies from your next billing period. A yearly plan keeps its price until the end of the year you have paid for.

Late payment. If an invoice is unpaid 14 days after it is due, we may pause the service until it is paid. We may also charge interest under the Late Payment of Commercial Debts (Interest) Act 1998.

How long a plan runs and how to cancel

Basics paid monthly runs month to month. You can cancel at any time and the plan ends at the end of the month you have paid for.

Basics or Advanced paid yearly runs for the year you have paid for. You can cancel at any time to stop it renewing. We do not refund part of a year.

Advanced paid monthly has a minimum term of three months. After that it runs month to month and either of us can end it on 30 days' written notice.

Consulting runs for the term in the proposal.

Unless you cancel, a plan renews for another period of the same length at the fee then shown on the pricing page.

Data

You own the data in your accounts and the data you give us. We use it only to provide the service to you. How we collect, store and delete it is set out in our privacy policy and on our data deletion page.

Where we handle personal data on your behalf we act as your processor, under the data processing terms we agree with you.

We keep a record of the recommendations we make across our clients and what happened when they were acted on. We remove anything that identifies you or your customers from that record before it is combined with other clients' records. That de-identified record belongs to us, and we use it to improve the advice we give every client. It never identifies you or your business.

We do not sell or share your data, except with the service providers named in our privacy policy who help us run the service.

What we never do without your instruction

Without your instruction we do not change anything in your ad accounts, contact your customers or post on your behalf. We never share your data beyond the service providers named in our privacy policy. We never take payment from an ad platform for advice we give you.

We use them only to provide the service and share them only with the service providers named in our privacy policy, under written contracts that keep them confidential. We will disclose your confidential information to no one else unless you instruct us or the law requires it.
