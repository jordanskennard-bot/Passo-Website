Pricing

Start free, then pay monthly. No hidden fees.

Plans bought in the Shopify app are billed by Shopify on your Shopify invoice. You can change or cancel your plan in the app at any time.

Plans

Start with the Free check in the Shopify app.

Free check, free
A health check of your Google Ads account, checked against your Shopify sales, with the top fixes first.
Basics, coming soon
Everything in the Free check, plus a monthly report, your top recommendations each month and alerts. You make the changes.
Advanced, by quote
Everything in Basics, and we make the changes you approve, with tests and a monthly call. Not in the Shopify app yet; email us to start.

Basics will be billed by Shopify in US dollars once it opens in the app. Prices do not include your advertising spend, which you pay to Google or Meta directly. Advanced and work outside Shopify are invoiced directly.
