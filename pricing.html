Questions

How am I billed?

Basics is billed through Shopify on your Shopify invoice. Shopify charges in US dollars ($129 a month) so the pound price is an approximate equivalent. We invoice Advanced and Enterprise directly in pounds.

How does paying yearly work?

You pay for the year upfront at 20% less than the monthly price. A yearly plan runs for the full year.

Does the fee include my ad spend?

No. You pay Google and Meta for your ads directly and pay us a monthly fee.

What access do you need?

Basics reads your accounts and changes nothing. Advanced asks for permission to make changes and you approve each one before it goes live.

Does creative production cost extra?

Yes. Concepts and briefs are part of Advanced. Images and video are billed per piece and creator costs are passed on at cost.

I have a health check code. Where do I use it?

Use the link in the invite we sent you. The health check is by code only for now.
