Questions

What people ask before they start.

How do I get a health check?

The health check is free and by invite for now. Email us and we will send you a code. You give us read-only access to Google Ads and Meta and the check is ready within 3 working days.

What access do you need?

The health check and Basics only read your accounts. Advanced asks for permission to make changes and you approve each one before it goes live.

How am I billed?

Basics is billed by Shopify in US dollars on your Shopify invoice. Shopify shows UK merchants an estimate in pounds. Advanced and Enterprise are invoiced by us directly. All prices exclude VAT.

How does yearly billing work?

Pay for the year upfront and save 10% on the monthly price. A yearly plan runs for the full year.

Do you take a percentage of my ad spend?

No. Every plan is a flat fee. The Advanced fee is set by the band your monthly ad spend sits in.

Can I cancel?

Basics on monthly billing can be cancelled at any time in the Shopify app. Advanced has a three month minimum, then runs monthly.

Does creative production cost extra?

Yes. Concepts and briefs are part of Advanced. Images and video are billed per piece, and creator costs are passed on at cost.

When does Basics open?

Soon. Join the waitlist and we will tell you when it opens in the Shopify app.
